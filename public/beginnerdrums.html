COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Beginner Drums is designed for students with little or no previous drumming experience.

Throughout this eight-week program, students will develop the fundamental skills required to play the drum kit confidently and musically.

Lessons combine technique, rhythm training, coordination exercises, music reading and practical playing so students learn both the mechanics and musical application of drumming.

8-WEEK PROGRAM

Course Curriculum

01
WEEK 1 Getting Started With Drums

Introduction to the drum kit and its components.

  • Understanding the drum kit
  • Correct sitting position
  • Holding drumsticks
  • Basic stick control
  • Introduction to rhythm

WEEK 2 Rhythm Foundations
  • Quarter notes
  • Eighth notes
  • Basic rests
  • Counting rhythm

  • Single-stroke roll
  • Double-stroke roll

  • Accent exercises

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous drumming experience?

No. This course is designed specifically for complete beginners.

Do I need my own drum kit?

Having access to a drum kit is recommended, but students can begin many exercises using drumsticks and a practice pad.

Can I take the course online?

Yes. Students can select Online or In-Person instruction during registration.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course requirements will receive a Vizag JamHub Certificate of Completion.

What happens after Beginner Drums?

Students can continue into the Intermediate Drums program after successfully completing the beginner level.
