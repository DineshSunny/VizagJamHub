COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Intermediate Guitar is designed for students who already understand basic chords, strumming patterns and fundamental guitar technique.

Throughout this eight-week program, students will strengthen rhythm playing, chord transitions, scales, lead guitar technique and musical coordination.

Lessons combine music theory, practical exercises, technique development and song performance so students can progress toward confident intermediate musicianship.

02

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You'll Learn

Intermediate Guitar Technique
Improve fretting-hand accuracy, picking control, finger strength and coordination through structured intermediate guitar exercises.
Barre Chords
Learn major and minor barre-chord shapes and develop the strength required to move them confidently across the fretboard.
Chord Progressions
Understand common chord progressions and learn how chords function together within different keys and songs.
Major & Minor Scales
Develop major, minor and pentatonic scale patterns while improving fretboard awareness and melodic control.
Rhythm & Strumming
Develop stronger rhythm through syncopation, subdivisions, accents, muting and varied strumming patterns.
Lead Guitar
Learn essential lead-guitar techniques including hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends and vibrato.
Improvisation
Use scales, chord tones and simple melodic ideas to begin creating your own lead-guitar phrases.
Song Performance
Apply rhythm, chords, lead techniques and musical expression while performing complete songs.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous guitar experience?

Yes. Students should already understand common open chords, basic strumming patterns and fundamental guitar technique.

How many classes are included each week?

Students attend three one-hour classes: Theory & Fundamentals, Practical Training and Song Class.

Will I learn barre chords and lead guitar?

Yes. The course includes barre chords, scales, lead-guitar techniques and introductory improvisation.

Do I need my own guitar?

Yes. Regular access to an acoustic or electric guitar is required for practice between lessons.

Can I take this course online?

Yes. Select Online and choose your preferred time and weekly batch.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course receive a Certificate of Completion.
