About This Course

The Intermediate Vocals course is designed for singers who already understand basic breathing, pitch and vocal exercises and want to develop greater control and confidence.

During this eight-week program, students will work on vocal range, resonance, pitch accuracy, dynamics, phrasing, ear training and complete song performance.

What You Will Learn

Breath Control

Develop stronger breath support for sustained and controlled singing.

Vocal Range

Expand your usable vocal range through structured exercises.

Pitch Accuracy

Improve intonation and maintain accurate notes throughout songs.

Vocal Resonance

Develop a fuller and more controlled vocal tone using resonance techniques.

Vocal Phrasing

Learn how to shape musical phrases with timing, dynamics and expression.

Ear Training

Strengthen your ability to recognize notes, intervals and melodic patterns.

Expression

Develop emotional delivery while maintaining healthy vocal technique.

Performance Skills

Build confidence for performing complete songs in front of others.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Vocal Foundation Review

Review posture, breathing, warm-ups, pitch control and healthy vocal production.

Week 2

Breath Support & Control

Develop stronger breath support, sustained notes and controlled phrase endings.

Week 3

Range Development

Extend the comfortable vocal range while maintaining tone, pitch and healthy technique.

Week 4

Resonance & Tone

Explore resonance placement, tone quality and vocal consistency across different registers.

Week 5

Pitch & Ear Training

Improve pitch accuracy through interval exercises, melodic patterns and listening practice.

Week 6

Dynamics & Vocal Phrasing

Use dynamics, articulation, timing and phrasing to create expressive vocal performances.

Week 7

Song Interpretation

Apply vocal technique and expression to complete songs while developing individual style.

Week 8

Final Vocal Performance

Prepare and perform a complete song demonstrating improved technique, control and expression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course suitable for complete beginners?

This course is intended for students who already have some basic singing experience and understand fundamental breathing and pitch exercises.

Will this course help improve my vocal range?

Yes. Range development is included, with emphasis on healthy and controlled vocal technique.

Will I perform complete songs?

Yes. Song interpretation and complete vocal performance are important parts of the course.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Course Policies

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Missed classes may not always be rescheduled and depend on instructor availability.

Students should complete assigned vocal exercises between classes.

Course fees are applicable to the selected eight-week batch and class format.
