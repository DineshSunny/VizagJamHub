COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Intermediate Vocals is designed for students who already understand the basic foundations of singing and want to develop greater vocal control, confidence and musical expression.

During this eight-week program, students will work on breath support, pitch accuracy, vocal range, resonance, register transitions, dynamics, phrasing and ear training.

The course combines vocal theory, guided practical exercises and weekly song classes so students can apply each technique directly to real music.

02

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You'll Learn

Breath Support & Control
Develop stronger breath support and learn to manage airflow efficiently while singing longer phrases and sustained notes.
Pitch Accuracy
Improve pitch recognition and develop greater accuracy while singing scales, melodies and complete songs.
Vocal Range
Expand your usable vocal range through structured exercises while maintaining healthy and controlled vocal production.
Resonance & Tone
Explore resonance, vocal placement and vowel shaping to develop a clearer, fuller and more consistent tone.
Register Transitions
Develop smoother transitions between different areas of your voice and improve coordination throughout your vocal range.
Ear Training
Strengthen your musical ear through pitch matching, interval recognition and melodic listening exercises.
Dynamics & Phrasing
Learn to control volume, intensity, articulation and breathing to shape musical phrases more effectively.
Vocal Performance
Build confidence while performing complete songs with improved technique, timing, expression and vocal control.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous singing experience?

Basic previous singing experience is recommended because this course builds upon fundamental breathing, pitch and vocal techniques.

How many classes are included each week?

Students attend three one-hour classes: Theory & Fundamentals, Practical Training and Song Class.

Will this course help improve my vocal range?

Yes. The course includes structured exercises for range development, register coordination and healthy vocal production.

Will I learn complete songs?

Yes. Every week includes a Song Class where students apply the techniques learned during the week to a selected song.

Can I take this course online?

Yes. Select Online and choose your preferred class time and weekly batch during enrollment.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course receive a Certificate of Completion.
