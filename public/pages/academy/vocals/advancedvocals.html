About This Course

The Advanced Vocals course is designed for experienced singers who already have a strong foundation in pitch, breathing, vocal control and song performance.

During the eight-week program, students will develop advanced breath management, register transitions, resonance, range, dynamics, vocal agility, interpretation and stage performance skills.

What You Will Learn

Advanced Breath Control

Develop efficient breath management for demanding vocal passages and sustained phrases.

Vocal Range

Strengthen and expand your usable vocal range while maintaining healthy technique.

Register Control

Develop smoother transitions between chest, head and mixed vocal registers.

Vocal Agility

Improve speed, flexibility and accuracy through advanced vocal exercises and melodic patterns.

Dynamics

Control volume, intensity and tonal variation without losing vocal stability.

Advanced Phrasing

Shape melodies using timing, articulation, dynamics and stylistic interpretation.

Vocal Expression

Develop individual style and communicate emotion through controlled vocal performance.

Stage Performance

Develop stage confidence, microphone technique and professional performance skills.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Advanced Vocal Technique

Evaluate vocal technique and strengthen breath support, posture, tone and vocal control.

Week 2

Range & Register Development

Strengthen chest, head and mixed registers while developing smoother register transitions.

Week 3

Resonance & Tone Control

Explore resonance placement, tonal balance and controlled changes in vocal color.

Week 4

Vocal Agility

Develop speed and accuracy through runs, melodic patterns, interval exercises and articulation.

Week 5

Dynamics & Advanced Phrasing

Use controlled dynamics, articulation and rhythmic placement to shape vocal phrases.

Week 6

Style & Interpretation

Explore stylistic choices, emotional delivery and individual interpretation within songs.

Week 7

Stage & Microphone Technique

Develop microphone control, stage movement, audience connection and live performance confidence.

Week 8

Advanced Final Performance

Prepare and perform a complete advanced song demonstrating vocal technique, interpretation and stage presence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should join the Advanced Vocals course?

The course is designed for experienced singers who already have good pitch, breath control and song-performance skills.

Will the course help expand my vocal range?

Yes. The course includes advanced range and register development while maintaining healthy vocal technique.

Will I learn stage performance?

Yes. Stage presence, microphone technique, interpretation and live performance are included in the course.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Course Policies
