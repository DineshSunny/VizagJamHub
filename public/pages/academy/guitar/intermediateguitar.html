About This Course

The Intermediate Guitar course is designed for students who already understand basic chords, rhythm patterns and simple songs and are ready to develop stronger musical control.

During the eight-week program, students will improve chord transitions, rhythm accuracy, scales, fretboard knowledge, lead guitar techniques and complete song performance.

What You Will Learn

Barre Chords

Develop clean major and minor barre chords across the fretboard.

Advanced Rhythm

Improve strumming accuracy, dynamics, accents and rhythmic control.

Scales

Learn major, minor and pentatonic scale patterns for practical guitar playing.

Lead Guitar

Develop bends, slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs and basic lead phrasing.

Fretboard Knowledge

Understand notes, intervals and chord relationships across the neck.

Ear Training

Improve your ability to recognize chords, rhythm and melodic patterns.

Song Playing

Apply techniques to complete songs across different musical styles.

Performance Skills

Develop timing and confidence for playing with singers and musicians.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Rhythm & Technique Review

Strengthen chord transitions, timing, alternate picking and rhythmic accuracy.

Week 2

Barre Chords

Major and minor barre chord shapes, movable chords and practical progressions.

Week 3

Major & Minor Scales

Scale construction, common positions and melodic exercises.

Week 4

Pentatonic Guitar

Minor pentatonic patterns, sequencing and introductory improvisation.

Week 5

Lead Guitar Techniques

Bending, sliding, hammer-ons, pull-offs and vibrato.

Week 6

Chord Extensions & Rhythm

Seventh chords, suspended chords, rhythmic variations and dynamics.

Week 7

Improvisation & Song Application

Combine scales, chords and techniques within complete songs.

Week 8

Final Performance

Prepare and perform a complete guitar piece demonstrating the skills developed during the course.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course suitable for complete beginners?

No. This course assumes that you already understand basic guitar chords, rhythm and simple song playing.

Can I use an acoustic guitar?

Yes. Both acoustic and electric guitars can be used for the Intermediate course.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Will I learn lead guitar?

Yes. The course introduces practical lead guitar techniques, scales and improvisation.

Course Policies

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Missed classes may not always be rescheduled and depend on instructor availability.

Students should complete assigned practice exercises between classes.

Course fees are applicable to the selected eight-week batch and class format.
