About This Course

The Advanced Guitar course is designed for experienced guitarists who already have strong rhythm, chord, scale and lead guitar foundations.

Students will work on advanced fretboard knowledge, extended chords, speed, articulation, improvisation, phrasing, complex rhythm and professional-level song performance.

What You Will Learn

Advanced Technique

Improve picking accuracy, speed, articulation and overall control.

Extended Chords

Explore seventh, ninth, suspended, altered and extended chord voicings.

Fretboard Mastery

Connect scales, intervals, chords and arpeggios across the fretboard.

Lead Guitar

Develop advanced bending, vibrato, legato, alternate picking and phrasing.

Advanced Scales

Apply major, minor, modal and pentatonic concepts musically.

Improvisation

Build expressive solos using melodic development and phrasing.

Complex Rhythm

Work with syncopation, subdivisions, rhythmic accents and changing grooves.

Performance

Develop confidence and musical communication for live performance.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Advanced Technique & Control

Picking efficiency, synchronization, speed development, articulation and technical accuracy.

Week 2

Fretboard Mastery

Connect notes, intervals, chord shapes and scale positions across the entire neck.

Week 3

Extended Chords & Voicings

Seventh, ninth and extended chords with practical chord substitutions and inversions.

Week 4

Advanced Scales & Modes

Major and minor scale relationships, modal sounds and practical fretboard application.

Week 5

Advanced Lead Guitar

Bending, vibrato, legato, alternate picking, slides and expressive articulation.

Week 6

Improvisation & Phrasing

Develop solos using motifs, dynamics, rhythmic variation and melodic phrasing.

Week 7

Advanced Song Arrangement

Apply rhythm, harmony, lead techniques and improvisation to complete musical arrangements.

Week 8

Advanced Final Performance

Prepare and perform a complete advanced guitar piece demonstrating technique, musicality and expression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should join the Advanced Guitar course?

This course is intended for guitarists who already have a strong understanding of chords, rhythm, scales and lead guitar.

Can I use an acoustic guitar?

Yes. Acoustic and electric guitars can both be used, although some advanced techniques may be easier to explore on an electric guitar.

Will the course cover improvisation?

Yes. Improvisation, phrasing and musical expression are important parts of the Advanced course.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Course Policies

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Missed classes may not always be rescheduled and depend on instructor availability.

Students should complete assigned practice exercises between classes.

Course fees are applicable to the selected eight-week batch and class format.
