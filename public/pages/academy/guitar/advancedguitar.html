COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Advanced Guitar is designed for experienced guitarists who already have a strong foundation in chords, scales, rhythm and lead guitar.

Throughout this eight-week program, students will develop advanced technique, fretboard knowledge, extended chords, scales, modes, improvisation and professional-level musical expression.

Lessons combine advanced theory, practical exercises, song arrangement and performance training to help students develop greater control, creativity and confidence as guitarists.

02

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You'll Learn

Advanced Guitar Technique
Develop greater speed, accuracy, control and coordination through advanced picking, fretting and technical exercises.
Fretboard Mastery
Understand note locations, intervals, chord tones and scale relationships across the entire fretboard.
Extended Chords
Learn seventh chords, suspended chords, added-tone chords and advanced voicings for richer harmonic playing.
Advanced Scales & Modes
Develop fluency with major, minor, pentatonic and modal scale concepts across multiple fretboard positions.
Advanced Lead Guitar
Refine bends, vibrato, legato, slides, alternate picking and expressive lead-guitar techniques.
Improvisation
Build expressive solos using scales, chord tones, motifs, dynamics and advanced melodic phrasing.
Song Arrangement
Arrange rhythm, harmony and lead parts into complete guitar performances.
Performance Skills
Develop musical confidence, stage awareness, timing and expressive control for live and recorded performance.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should join Advanced Guitar?

This course is designed for experienced guitarists who already understand chords, scales, rhythm and basic lead-guitar techniques.

How many classes are included each week?

Students attend three one-hour classes: Theory & Fundamentals, Practical Training and Song Class.

Will I learn advanced improvisation?

Yes. The course covers advanced scales, chord-tone targeting, phrasing and structured guitar improvisation.

Do I need my own guitar?

Yes. Regular access to an acoustic or electric guitar is required for practice between lessons.

Can I take this course online?

Yes. Select Online and choose your preferred time and weekly batch.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course receive a Certificate of Completion.
