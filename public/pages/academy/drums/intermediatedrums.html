COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Intermediate Drums is designed for students who already understand basic drum-kit setup, stick control, simple notation and foundational beats.

Throughout this eight-week program, students will strengthen coordination, timing, dynamics, groove vocabulary and musical control across the drum kit.

Lessons combine intermediate technique, syncopation, subdivisions, independence exercises, fills and practical song performance so students can play with greater control, consistency and musical expression.

02

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You'll Learn

Drum Kit Setup
Learn the main components of a drum kit, correct seating position, drum placement and how to arrange the kit comfortably.
Holding Drumsticks
Develop a comfortable drumstick grip while learning proper hand position, balance, rebound and control.
Reading Drum Notation
Understand basic note values, rests, counting, measures and how drum-kit parts are represented in written music.
Basic Rudiments
Practice single strokes, double strokes, paradiddles and accents while developing control between both hands.
Rock Beats
Combine the hi-hat, snare and bass drum to develop foundational rock beats and basic hand-and-foot coordination.
Musical Fills & Transitions
Learn how drum fills connect sections of a song and practice moving smoothly around the snare and toms.
Groove Development
Develop consistent timing, dynamics and groove while learning to play steadily alongside music.
Practice Techniques
Build effective practice routines using repetition, metronome exercises and gradual tempo development.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous drumming experience?

Yes. Students should already be comfortable with basic stick control, simple drum beats, counting and fundamental drum-kit coordination.

How many classes are included each week?

Students attend three one-hour classes: Theory & Fundamentals, Practical Training and Song Class.

What happens during Song Class?

Students apply what they learned during the week to a Hindi, Telugu or English song.

Do I need my own drum kit?

A drum kit is recommended, but many exercises can begin with drumsticks and a practice pad.

Can I take this course online?

Yes. Select Online and choose your preferred time and weekly batch.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course receive a Certificate of Completion.
