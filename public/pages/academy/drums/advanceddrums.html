COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Advanced Drums is designed for experienced drummers who already have a strong understanding of fundamental rhythms, grooves, fills and drum-kit coordination.

Throughout this eight-week program, students will develop advanced independence, speed, control, musical vocabulary and performance confidence.

Lessons combine advanced technique, complex rhythmic concepts, improvisation, groove development and practical song performance.

02

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You'll Learn

Advanced Technique
Develop greater speed, control, endurance and dynamic range through advanced hand and foot exercises.
Four-Way Independence
Strengthen independence between both hands and feet while performing increasingly complex rhythmic patterns.
Advanced Rhythms
Explore syncopation, subdivisions, odd groupings and advanced rhythmic phrasing.
Advanced Rudiments
Develop advanced rudimental vocabulary and apply patterns creatively across the entire drum kit.
Complex Grooves
Build sophisticated grooves using ghost notes, accents, displacement and dynamic control.
Linear Drumming
Develop linear patterns where individual limbs create flowing and intricate rhythmic phrases.
Improvisation
Develop creative improvisation skills while maintaining musical structure, timing and dynamics.
Performance Skills
Prepare advanced songs and develop the consistency, confidence and musical awareness required for live performance.

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous drumming experience?

Yes. Advanced Drums is intended for students who already have a strong foundation in drumming technique, grooves and coordination.

How many classes are included each week?

Students attend three one-hour classes: Theory & Fundamentals, Practical Training and Song Class.

What happens during Song Class?

Students apply advanced techniques and concepts to Hindi, Telugu or English songs.

Do I need my own drum kit?

Regular access to a drum kit is strongly recommended for Advanced Drums because consistent full-kit practice is important.

Can I take this course online?

Yes. Select Online and choose your preferred time and weekly batch.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible students who successfully complete the course receive a Certificate of Completion.
