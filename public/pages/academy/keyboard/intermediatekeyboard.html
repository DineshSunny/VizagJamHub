About This Course

The Intermediate Keyboard course is designed for students who already understand basic notes, chords, scales and simple songs and are ready to develop stronger keyboard technique.

During this eight-week program, students will improve two-hand coordination, chord progressions, scales, inversions, accompaniment patterns, rhythm and complete song performance.

What You Will Learn

Two-Hand Coordination

Develop stronger independence and coordination between both hands.

Chord Progressions

Learn commonly used progressions and apply them to complete songs.

Major & Minor Scales

Build stronger scale technique across multiple keys.

Chord Inversions

Use inversions to create smoother chord movement and accompaniment.

Rhythm Patterns

Develop rhythmic accuracy using different accompaniment patterns.

Music Theory

Understand intervals, chord construction and key relationships.

Song Playing

Apply chords, melody and rhythm to complete keyboard arrangements.

Performance Skills

Develop timing and confidence for playing independently and with others.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Keyboard Foundation Review

Review notes, basic chords, scales, fingering and rhythm while strengthening technique.

Week 2

Major & Minor Chords

Strengthen major and minor chord construction and apply chords across different keys.

Week 3

Chord Inversions

Learn first and second inversions and use them to create smooth chord transitions.

Week 4

Scale Development

Practice major and minor scales with improved fingering, timing and two-hand coordination.

Week 5

Accompaniment Patterns

Develop left-hand and right-hand accompaniment patterns for different styles of music.

Week 6

Melody & Harmony

Combine melody and chord harmony using both hands in practical keyboard arrangements.

Week 7

Song Arrangement

Apply chords, inversions, rhythm and melody to complete song arrangements.

Week 8

Final Keyboard Performance

Prepare and perform a complete keyboard piece demonstrating technique, coordination and musical expression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course suitable for complete beginners?

No. Students should already understand basic keyboard notes, chords, scales and simple song playing.

Do I need my own keyboard?

Yes. Access to a keyboard or piano is important for regular practice between classes.

Will I learn to play with both hands?

Yes. Two-hand coordination, melody, harmony and accompaniment are important parts of the Intermediate course.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Course Policies

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Missed classes may not always be rescheduled and depend on instructor availability.

Students should complete assigned keyboard exercises between classes.

Course fees are applicable to the selected eight-week batch and class format.
