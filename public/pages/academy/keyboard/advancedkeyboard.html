COURSE INTRODUCTION

About This Course

Advanced Keyboard is designed for experienced students who already have strong control of basic and intermediate keyboard techniques.

Throughout this eight-week program, students explore advanced harmony, extended chords, voicings, improvisation, transposition, arrangement and expressive performance.

Lessons combine advanced theory, practical keyboard work and complete song performance to develop versatile and confident musicianship.

Two-Hand Coordination
