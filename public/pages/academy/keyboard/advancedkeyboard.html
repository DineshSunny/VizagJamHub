About This Course

The Advanced Keyboard course is designed for experienced keyboard players who already have a strong understanding of chords, scales, rhythm and two-hand playing.

During this eight-week program, students will develop advanced harmony, extended chord voicings, improvisation, accompaniment, two-hand independence, arrangement and performance skills.

What You Will Learn

Advanced Technique

Develop stronger finger control, accuracy, speed and keyboard fluency.

Hand Independence

Build advanced coordination between independent left and right-hand parts.

Advanced Chords

Learn seventh, ninth and extended chord structures and voicings.

Advanced Harmony

Understand chord relationships, substitutions and harmonic movement.

Advanced Scales

Develop fluency with major, minor and modal scale concepts.

Improvisation

Create melodic ideas using scales, chord tones and musical phrasing.

Arrangement

Create complete keyboard arrangements using melody, harmony and rhythm.

Performance Skills

Develop musical confidence for solo, band and live performance settings.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1

Advanced Technique & Coordination

Strengthen finger independence, two-hand coordination, accuracy, speed and rhythmic control.

Week 2

Extended Chords

Explore seventh, ninth and extended chord structures, inversions and practical voicings.

Week 3

Advanced Harmony

Study chord relationships, harmonic movement, substitutions and voice leading.

Week 4

Advanced Scales & Modes

Develop major, minor and modal scale knowledge and apply it across the keyboard.

Week 5

Advanced Accompaniment

Develop sophisticated accompaniment patterns, rhythmic textures and left-hand approaches.

Week 6

Improvisation & Phrasing

Create melodic ideas using chord tones, scales, motifs and rhythmic phrasing.

Week 7

Advanced Song Arrangement

Combine melody, harmony, accompaniment and improvisation into complete keyboard arrangements.

Week 8

Advanced Final Performance

Prepare and perform a complete advanced keyboard piece demonstrating technique, musicality and expression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should join the Advanced Keyboard course?

This course is designed for keyboard players who already understand chords, scales, rhythm, inversions and two-hand playing.

Do I need my own keyboard?

Yes. Regular access to a keyboard or piano is important for practicing the advanced techniques taught in this course.

Will I learn improvisation?

Yes. The course includes improvisation, advanced harmony, chord voicings and melodic phrasing.

How many classes are included?

The course includes 24 classes over eight weeks, with three classes each week.

Course Policies

Students are expected to attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Missed classes may not always be rescheduled and depend on instructor availability.

Students should complete assigned keyboard exercises between classes.

Course fees are applicable to the selected eight-week batch and class format.
